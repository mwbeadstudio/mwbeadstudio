Couples' Sterling Silver Pixiu, Hematite & Onyx Bracelets

A meaningful pair to share: sterling silver Pixiu details meet metallic hematite and matte black onyx, bringing together symbols of prosperity, protection, and lasting strength.

Sterling silver Pixiu
In Chinese tradition, Pixiu symbolizes wealth, protection, and good fortune, with the hope of positive transformation through challenging times.
Hematite
Traditionally associated with grounding, stability, and protection, hematite represents a steady presence and a focused mind.
Black onyx
A symbol of protection, inner strength, and resilience, black onyx represents staying grounded, determined, and clear in purpose.
