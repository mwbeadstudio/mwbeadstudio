# Product photo watermarks

Use `images/bracelet_2.png` as the visual reference for product photo branding.

- Text: `MW Bead Studio` in thin, elegant cursive.
- Pattern: repeating diagonal lines rising to the right, in staggered rows.
- Appearance: faint neutral gray, approximately 20% opacity.
- Scale: each text repeat is approximately 40% of the image width; row spacing is approximately 22% of the image height.
- Preserve the photograph's aspect ratio, jewelry details, colors, and composition.
- Replace previous watermark lettering rather than adding a second pattern over it.
- Keep source images unchanged. Website-ready copies live in `images/watermarked/`.
- Use the same image file wherever a product appears, including homepage features, gallery cards, full-size links, and the Our Story page.
